ZHANG Xiaoru1 LI Daqi2 SHI Shuqing3 HE Jing4 WANG Ran5 LIU Wei6 ZHANG Yuanqing7 1.Institute Office, Guangwai Hospital, Xicheng District, Beijing 100055, China; 2.Medical Insurance Office, Guang ’ anmen Hospital, China A cademy of Chinese Medical Sciences, Beijing 100053, China; 3.Department of Cardiovascular, Guang ’ anmen Hospital, China Academy of Chinese Medical Sciences, Beijing 100053, China; 4.Department of Medical, Guangwai Hospital, Xicheng District, Beijing 100055, China; 5.Department of Information, Guangwai Hospital, Xicheng District, Beijing 100055, China; 6.West Pharmacy, Guangwai Hospital, Xicheng District, Beijing 100055, China; 7.Chinese Pharmacy, Guangwai Hospital, Xicheng District, Beijing 100055, China[Abstract] Objective To improve the level of the combined application of Chinese and western medicine by establishing the interaction rules and management system of pre-prescription review in hospital. Methods The problems existed in the pre-prescription review of the combination application of Chinese and western medicine were found through interviews, and the unreasonable combined use database of Chinese and western medicine was established through literatures and other materials, and the rules of the pre-prescription review were set up according to the database. The outpatient and emergency prescriptions of Guangwai Hospital, Xicheng District of Beijing, from January to December 2022 were obtained, and the drug interaction rules and management system were optimized by PDCA cycle method, and the rationality level of combined application of Chinese and western medicine before and after optimization was compared. Results A database of 57 kinds of 147 kinds of Chinese medicine and 61 kinds of 148 kinds of western medicine was established. After four rounds of PDCA cycle management and the establishment of pre-review interaction rules and management system for the combination of western and Chinese medicine was established, the proportion of unreasonable prescriptions for the combination of traditional Chinese and western medicine decreased from 9.11% to 2.44%, the modification rate of intervention prescription by physicians increased from 82.93% to 95.45%, and the average assessment score of physicians increased from 79.30 scores to 94.25 scores. Conclusion Through the establishment of the prescription rules for the irrational combination application of Chinese and western medicine in the pre-prescription review, the level of irrational combination of Chinese and western medicine can be improved and the risk caused by irrational combination can be reduced.[Key words] Pre-prescription review; Combination application of Chinese and western medicine; Drug interaction; Database中医西医分属不同体系，中西药联用缺乏临床系统配伍研究，导致不合理配伍应用疗效降低，甚至产生不良反应，引发药源性疾病[1-4] 因严重药物相互作用致住院的人数占总住院人数的3%[5] 药物相互作用审核，对保障临床安全用药十分重要, 医院内应建立完善的中西药联用的管理和监督体系[6-7]国家卫生健康委员会 2018 年 7 月发布《医疗机构处方审核规范》[8]对前置处方审核及信息化提出要求，大部分三甲医院已上线处方前置审核系统，但该工作刚起步，制度尚不健全，审核系统药物相互作用规则主要针对两种及两种以上西药联用，中西药合用的审核规则尚未建立[9] 本研究旨在探索建立中西药联合用药的前置处方审核规则50名）等多角度改进中西药不合理联用规则，建立医师-药师-信息工程师-医务部门沟通机制对新修订的规则制度，医师药师信息工程师医务职能部门同步展开培训 ③check：二季度开始，多学科团队每月汇总中西药不合理处方情况，动态监测观察指标，包括不合理联用处方数、不合理联用处方占比、干预处方医师修改率、医务人员考核加权平均分（季度加权平均分 $= X _ { 1 } \times 2 0 \% + X _ { 2 } \times 3 0 \% + X _ { 3 } \times 5 0 \% / 2 0 \% + 3 0 \% + 5 0 \% \times 1 . 1 \times 1 . 2 \times X _ { 3 }$ 代表每季度第 1\~3 个月的分数） 以监测内容为绩效考核指标进行考核。 ④action：查看第二季度中西药不合理联用，分析仍存在的问题，优化不合理联用规则，进入第二轮循环，对2022年4 月至6 月规则进行优化。 之后实施第三轮、第四轮，对2022 年7 月至 9月 10月至12月的不合理联用规则进行优化

2 1 多学科团队访谈问题的优化整改

3 2 前置处方审核中西药不合理联用规则设定
以患者 ID 识别用药，进行药物相互作用规则设置，可实现系统自动识别患者用药，黑灯拦截的药物弹窗提醒，医师必须修改用药方可通过；红灯提示的药物，弹窗提醒医师修改或双签字改变了以往中西药独立审核，无法自动识别中西药联用是否合理的现状经过信息系统调整和前置处方审核药物相互作用规则的设置，实现中西药联用合理性的提高
3 3 前置处方审核中西药联用规则存在的问题
一是中西药联用既要考虑药物成分，也要兼顾剂型给药方式给药时间个体化差异，这对规则的精细化设置提出要求二是中西医中西药分属不同知识体系非本专业知识的欠缺，导致多学科团队发现解决临床中西药不合理联用存在难度医师药师需注重合理用药知识积累，不确定的用药及时查阅文献，寻找循证依据三是前置处方审核规则设置需根据医院药品变化及时调整对证据不充分的药物，可通过真实世界研究临床观察指标监测等方式重点关注，为中西药不合理联用提供计算机系统和临床研究依据规则的完善有赖中西药相互作用研究不断深入
